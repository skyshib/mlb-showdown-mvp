Where people are playing

By the clock on their own machine — the one location signal that is not a guess made about somebody, and the one a VPN or a phone carrier cannot move. A zone is a region, not a city: Seattle, Portland, San Diego and Las Vegas all keep Pacific time, and none of them is Los Angeles.

    What the addresses say

    The same people as read off their IP, which is a third party's guess: mobile resolves to the carrier's gateway, and a relay resolves to wherever it happens to exit. City-level where the provider can manage it — freeipapi, or ipinfo when IPINFO_TOKEN is set.
