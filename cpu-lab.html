What it is worth, room by room

One dot per room: what the managers carrying the tweak won there, as their own win% against the same seats in a room where nobody carried it, averaged over how many of them had it. Every point is relative by construction — the seats of one room average out between them, which is why some chairs stay untweaked, and why this says a tweak is better IN A MIXED ROOM rather than that a table of them would be better.

One dot per room, in alphabetical order, in the same place in every panel — so a column can be read down the page to compare one room across candidates. A dot is that room's average over the adoption levels measured in it, weighted equally, which is what the number above the chart adds up to. Hover a dot for the room, its interval and how many levels are behind it.
